## Scope

I2S controller registers, FIFO and DMA hookup, and clocking through the MPMU audio tree ([01](01-clocks-resets.md) §1.6.4).

DT compatible `spacemit,k3-i2s`. There are six instances, i2s0–i2s5.

Clock and reset IDs for these blocks are in [01](01-clocks-resets.md).
